Executive Finding: CANOD's engineering foundation is exceptionally rigorous—its computational permutation engines, Canadian regulatory compliance references (CSA, ESA, Health Canada, CATSA), and technical accuracy are best-in-class. However, several existing articles and tools suffer from cognitive friction and protocol-first framing (e.g. demanding users identify "DisplayPort Alt Mode" or calculate RAID parity). CANOD must pivot from an "engineer's compliance manual" into an "instant problem solver" where technical terminology strictly follows simple, 30-second answers.

PART 4 — FINAL STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

A. Best 5 Existing Articles

  • 1. Why Is My Phone Charging Slowly? Flagship article. 15-minute diagnostic test, room-temp checks, lint inspection. Absolute benchmark for the site.
  • 2. Why Is My Wi-Fi Slow in One Room? Isolates single-device issues from building layout and router placement before pushing hardware.
  • 3. What Size Power Bank Do I Need? Solves the 10,000 mAh vs real watt-hours dilemma and clarifies Canadian airport 100 Wh rules.
  • 4. Why USB-C Cables Work Differently: Demystifies identical cables using the relatable Apple charge cable vs Thunderbolt comparison.
  • 5. Do I Need a Router or Mesh System? Saves families hundreds of dollars by helping them avoid unnecessary 3-pack mesh systems.

B. Worst 5 Existing Articles

  • 1. Choosing a Home NAS: Reads like an enterprise IT guide (RAID parity, diskless bays, 1GbE ceilings). Completely misaligned with ordinary consumers.
  • 2. Seven Things Before Buying a USB-C Dock: Paralyzes users with DP Alt Mode, MST, and DisplayLink permissions before solving their monitor setup.
  • 3. Before Buying a Charger in Canada: Valuable safety points, but reads like an ESA compliance brief citing Ontario rules.
  • 4. Power Bar or Surge Protector: Gets bogged down in joule clamping voltages and provincial bulletins rather than simple buying rules.
  • 5. Choosing a Tech Organizer: Good advice, but lowest consumer problem urgency and thinnest search volume on the site.

C. Best Existing Tool

USB-C Dock Compatibility Checker (/tools/usb-c-dock-checker/)

Its 25,200-permutation client-side engine in lib/dock-checker.ts is technically flawless. It accurately diagnoses Apple Silicon Mac dual-monitor mirroring and DisplayLink screen-recording permissions. Once protocol dropdowns are replaced by a simple laptop selector, it will be CANOD's most valuable tool.

D. Tools to Remove or Rebuild

  • REMOVE: NAS Capacity Calculator: Everyday consumers do not buy 4-bay NAS enclosures or configure RAID. Synology already provides a free RAID calculator. Replace with a Photo & Cloud Storage Cost Calculator.
  • REBUILD: Canadian Electrical Safety Checklist: Rebuild the 10-question self-report form into a 3-step visual "Safe or Scam Screener" with photos of genuine vs fake CSA marks.
